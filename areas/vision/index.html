Vision

AboutTreatments (5)

Eye focusing, eye tracking, and light sensitivity.

A concussion can change how your eyes work together.

Signs

  • Reading tires you out fast.
  • Words blur or seem to move.
  • Screens or bright lights bother you.

What can help

  • An eye exam that checks focusing and tracking. A normal sight test can miss these problems.
  • Eye exercises, guided by a specialist.
  • Dimmer screens and short breaks.

Who can help

An optometrist or eye doctor with concussion training.
